## Notes and deviations

- Ramp denominator: the Goal fixes `DRAM_READ_CAPACITY` to the measured capacity from `dram_read_bw`. Long single-CTA-per-SM streams (grids 112-132, >= 7 MiB/CTA) exceed that capacity by up to 2.4 %, so published fractions slightly above 1.0 are kept unclamped; they are correct only together with the same 4512.6 GB/s denominator (i.e. `dram_read_capacity_per_clk` = 1444.032 at 3.125 GHz). If the maintainer chooses a different capacity for the yaml, the ramp fractions must be rescaled by the ratio. `bw_per_sm.cu` cannot provide the capacity: one CTA/SM (256 or 1024 threads) reaches only 1.31 / 3.96 TB/s.
- Residency spread gate: the A800 runner aborts on any footprint with trial spread > 8 %. On H200 the 45-54 MiB transition exceeds it in every capture (54 MiB: 13-19 %), also with 256 instead of 5 warm-up passes (captures `l2_residency_knee_20260925_{a,b}_warmup5` and `_warmup256`, measurements retained). The H200 runner therefore records `spread_gate_exceeded` instead of aborting (non-hardware runner deviation, stated in its header); the published captures flag 54 MiB (a) and 51, 54 MiB (b). The probe itself keeps the A800 warm-up of 5.
- Residency exclusions: 6 and 12 MiB are excluded by the A800 "< 2 grid-stride iterations per thread" rule (12 MiB falls under it on H200 because the resident grid grows to 1056 CTAs).
- Mixed DRAM counter validation (A800 NCU read-miss/write-sector proof), SASS dumps and cuobjdump/NCU tool hashes are absent: NCU and cuobjdump are not installed on the capture host; the keys are kept with null values.
- Preflight: only the owned GPU 1 was checked idle (other GPUs belong to other workers), so `preflight_all_devices_idle` is recorded as false with `preflight_owned_device_idle: true`; capture PIDs were not recorded.
- The yaml `components[l2].size_bytes` comment says 50 MiB; the device reports 62914560 B (60 MiB), which all WP4 footprints were drawn against (outside WP4 key scope; noted for the maintainer).
- Incident: during the initial clock-behaviour check I ran `pkill -u <uid> -x nvidia-smi` on the capture host, which may have terminated another worker's concurrent `nvidia-smi -lms` sampler at 02:41 (remote time). All later runners stop only their own sampler PID.
